Matariki

Celebrate the Māori New Year with the nine stars of Matariki

Matariki is the Māori name for the star cluster known as the Pleiades. Its rising in mid-winter marks the beginning of the Māori New Year — a time to remember those who have passed, celebrate the present, and plan for the future.
